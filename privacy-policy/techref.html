Privacy Policy & Disclaimer

TechRef keeps your data on your device and backs it up only to your own iCloud account. Nothing you enter is ever sent to the Developer or to anyone else. This page explains exactly what that means, and the limits of how the App's output should be used.

Effective: October 13, 2026

1Introduction

This is the Privacy Policy and Disclaimer for TechRef, an iOS app developed by Flying Dev Lab (空飛ぶ研究室).

TechRef is a unit conversion, engineering reference and calculation tool for technical professionals. It is built on a simple principle: your data belongs to you. It stays on your device and in your own iCloud account, and the Developer never receives it.

Please read this page before using the App. The Disclaimer section describes important limitations on how the App's output should be used.

2Data Stored on Your Device

All data you enter stays on your device and in your own iCloud account. TechRef has no server of its own, and nothing is transmitted to the Developer.

TechRef stores the following on your device:

  • Conversion history (values, tolerances, units, labels, memos, timestamps)
  • Sheets data (part names, measurement point names, dimensions, measured values, memos)
  • App settings (decimal places, appearance, language, the units, tables, materials and other items you choose to show, and the units used in the calculators)
  • Information the App needs to work correctly (the number of free saves used, whether you have agreed to the terms, and which in-app tips have been shown)

Values entered in the calculators are kept only while the calculator is open and are not saved.

History and Sheets are saved as files in the App's Application Support directory. Settings are saved in the standard iOS preferences store. Deleting the App removes all of this data from your device. A backup in your iCloud account is not deleted with the App; see iCloud Backup.

The Developer has no access to any of it.

3Network Activity

TechRef's core features — unit conversion, tolerance calculation, the reference tables, the calculators, and saving your data — work entirely offline. No network connection is required to use them.

There are no analytics SDKs, no crash reporting services, no advertising SDKs, and no third-party libraries that transmit data.

The App itself connects to the network only in the following cases:

  • iCloud backup and restore — your Sheets, history and settings are sent to your own iCloud account, and read back from it when you restore (see iCloud Backup). Automatic backup is on by default and can be turned off in Settings.
  • Purchasing or restoring the in-app purchase — handled by Apple through the App Store (see In-App Purchases)
  • Tapping a link in the App — such as the Support page, this policy, or an official standards website listed in Settings — which opens in your browser

In none of these cases is your data sent to the Developer. iCloud backups go only to your own iCloud account. Purchase traffic goes to Apple and contains none of the values you entered in the App.

Usage statistics collected by Apple. Because the App is distributed through the App Store, Apple — as the platform operator — aggregates statistics such as installs, launches, session length, and purchases, and provides them to the Developer in anonymized, aggregated form. If you have enabled diagnostic data sharing in your iOS settings, crash reports are provided in the same way. All of this is managed by Apple, and the Developer can neither enable nor disable it.

What these statistics never include: the dimensions, tolerances, part names, measurement point names, measured values, labels, memos, conversion history, or Sheets content you enter. The only copy of your data that leaves your device is the backup in your own iCloud account, which the Developer cannot access. Apple's statistics tell the Developer that the App was used — not what you calculated.

Apart from the iCloud backup, your data leaves the device only through the clipboard copy and CSV export described in Exporting Your Data, and both happen only when you choose to do it.

4iCloud Backup

TechRef backs up your Sheets (including measured values), history and settings to your own iCloud account, using the private database of Apple's CloudKit. This lets you restore your data after reinstalling the App or moving to a new device. iCloud backup is free.

  • Where it is stored — in the private database of your iCloud account. It can be read only when you are signed in with your Apple Account. The Developer cannot see, access, or delete it.
  • When it is sent — with Back Up Automatically on (the default), shortly after you save and when you close the App. Back Up Now in Settings > iCloud backs up straight away. If you are not signed in to iCloud, nothing is backed up.
  • What is not backed up — the number of free saves used, your agreement to the terms, in-app tips, and your purchase. A purchase is restored from Apple separately (see In-App Purchases).
  • Restoring — after reinstalling the App or on a new device, the App asks whether to restore the backup. Restore from iCloud in Settings > iCloud replaces the data on the device with the backup.

Turning it off and deleting it. You can turn off Back Up Automatically in Settings > iCloud in the App. The backup uses your iCloud storage. To delete it, open Settings > [your name] > iCloud > Manage Account Storage on your device and choose TechRef.

Device backups. Separately from the above, if iCloud Backup is enabled in your device settings, iOS may include TechRef's data files in your device backup, along with data from your other apps. This is a standard operating system feature managed entirely by Apple. The Developer neither initiates nor has access to these backups.

Data stored in iCloud is handled under Apple's Privacy Policy.

5Exporting Your Data

TechRef lets you take your data out of the App in two ways:

  • Copy — conversion results, sheet data or measured values are placed on the system clipboard
  • Save to Files — a CSV file is written to a location you choose in the Files app

Both actions are initiated by you, and the App does not send data anywhere on its own. Once data leaves the App, its handling — including where you paste it, who you send it to, and any cloud storage location you select in the Files app — is outside the App's control and is your responsibility.

If you work with dimensional data that your organization treats as confidential, please follow your own internal policies when exporting, and when deciding whether to use iCloud backup.

6Advertising and Tracking

TechRef contains no advertisements.

The App does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA), does not request App Tracking Transparency permission, and does not track you across apps or websites. There is no profiling, no behavioral analysis, and no data broker relationship of any kind.

This is a design decision, not a temporary state. TechRef is a professional tool, and it will not carry advertising.

7In-App Purchases

TechRef is free to download. Unit conversion, tolerance calculation, the drill size tables, the cutting speed and feed calculator, measured values and iCloud backup can be used without any usage limit, whether or not you purchase.

Saving new dimensions to History and Sheets is free up to a certain number of saves. Beyond that, the frequency of saving is limited. A one-time (non-consumable) in-app purchase, TechRef Pro, removes that limit permanently and unlocks additional features such as further reference tables and the engineering calculators. The specific number, the nature of the limit, the features included, and the price are shown on the App Store product page and on the purchase screen inside the App.

All payment processing is handled by Apple through the App Store. The Developer will not receive your payment details, card information, or any personal information associated with the transaction. See Apple's Privacy Policy for how Apple handles that data.

Restoring a purchase. A purchase is tied to your Apple Account, not to a particular device, and it is not part of the iCloud backup. "Restore Purchases" in Settings asks Apple whether that account holds the purchase. The query is completed between your device and Apple; the Developer neither stores nor receives your Apple Account. Use it to restore your purchase after changing devices or reinstalling the App.

Any change of this kind will be reflected on this page before it takes effect.

8Sharing of Information

The Developer does not sell, share, or transfer user information to third parties, for the simple reason that no user information is collected in the first place.

There is no data to disclose to advertisers, analytics providers, or data brokers. Your iCloud backup is stored by Apple in your own iCloud account, and the Developer cannot access it. In the event of a legally binding request from a competent authority, the Developer has no user data that could be produced.

9Children's Privacy

TechRef is a professional engineering tool and is not directed at children.

Nevertheless, because the App collects no personal information from anyone of any age, it presents no children's privacy concerns. There is no data to protect, share, or delete — for any user.

Conversion factors, reference tables, material data, and calculated results provided by the App are reference information compiled with reference to officially published standards information and definitions. They are not certified data and carry no warranty of accuracy, completeness, or fitness for any particular purpose.

Verification is your responsibility. Before applying any value from the App to design, machining, inspection, or procurement, you must verify it against the applicable official standard, the controlling drawing, and your organization's internal procedures. The App is intended to assist that process, not to replace it.

Limits of the tolerance calculation. The center value and range shown by the App are the result of arithmetic on the values you enter. The calculation does not account for machining method, tooling condition, measurement uncertainty, material properties, thermal expansion, fit class, or any other physical or process factor. A mathematically valid center value is not necessarily an appropriate machine setting.

Limits of the engineering calculators. The calculators use simple textbook formulas under idealized conditions, such as a uniform section, linear elastic behavior, and the support and load conditions shown. They do not check buckling, fatigue, stress concentration, or other failure modes. Their results do not replace a strength check by a qualified person or an appropriate safety factor.

Material data. In the material tables, values marked with ≥ or ≤ are limits compiled with reference to officially published standards information. All other values, including density, Young's modulus, and thermal expansion, are representative values and are not guaranteed. Corrosion and chemical resistance ratings are TechRef's own relative ratings. Actual properties depend on the grade, condition, form, size, and supplier; check the mill certificate, the maker's data sheet, or the applicable standard.

Rounding and display precision. Results are rounded to the number of decimal places selected in Settings. Rounding may introduce error, and displayed values may differ from the full-precision internal result. Choose a precision appropriate to your tolerance band.

Unit selection. You are responsible for selecting the correct unit and category for your input. The App cannot detect an incorrect unit assignment.
